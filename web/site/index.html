Settings

The modal engine

0 dB

The free strings ringing with what is played, pedal down (0 dB: as measured).

100 %

How far apart the strings of each note are tuned: 100 % as measured, 0 % in perfect unison (no beating), 200 to 300 % an old upright's. The notes struck from then on.

50 %

A note struck again strikes its string while it rings, and the strikes add up; the hammer absorbs this share of the vibration (0 %: they add exactly; 50 %: fitted on the reference's repeated notes).

The room around the piano

The room, its preset and the listening position are in the bar above too.

0 dB

The room's reflections against what the physics gives (0 dB: as computed).

1.0 m

From the piano to the listener. 1 m: the engine's sound, heard from the keyboard; further, the direct sound falls, loses its highs to the air and narrows, and the room grows.

Headphones: the piano in front of you rather than inside your head. Each side of it reaches both ears, the farther one later and duller (a spherical head); so do the room's reflections. Room on or off.

Moving away changes the balance between the piano and the room, not the volume.

This room and this listening position under a name of your own (an existing name is replaced), listed under "My presets" in the room list. Delete removes the one selected there.

Velocity

The highest velocity the MIDI keyboard really sends (Korg B2: about 114): it then reaches the loudest layers.

Sustain pedal

Under it, the engine gets 0 (the dampers resting).

Over it, the engine gets 127 (the dampers lifted).

Where the half pedal falls in the pedal's travel.
